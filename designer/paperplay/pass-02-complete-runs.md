# Pass 02 — Complete Hostile Runs

These runs use the amendments proposed in Pass 01: +1 Influence/day, 15-Influence declaration, 8-Influence bargain/request, Leaning maturation, revised Oswin terms and guaranteed Renard vulnerability.

They are not scripted tutorials. They test whether a focused strategy can pay its costs, whether rivals have counterplay and whether the ending follows the constitution.

## Run A — Pure coalition attempt

### Objective

Win a four-vote first ballot with Mara, Edric and Ysabel while doing almost nothing for Claim or Church.

### Opening

The player spends the Stable phase conserving Gold and Influence. At elapsed Day 14 they hold approximately:

- 98 Gold;
- 49 Influence;
- 388–390 levies;
- 10 Claim;
- 12 Prestige.

### Sequence

1. Declare at Ailing: -15 Influence.
2. Open Mara's Greyfen Charter bargain and Edric's Border Aid in parallel: -16 Influence.
3. Apply Greyfen Charter immediately.
4. Lock 150 levies for Edric for seven days.
5. Both lords become Leaning when the bargains complete.
6. Wait the two-day Ailing maturation.
7. Request both declarations: -16 Influence.
8. Mara and Edric become Pledged.
9. After their public support is visible, offer Ysabel the Chancellorship package: -8 Influence and lock 40 Gold.
10. Ysabel's Viability Proof is satisfied by the two public Pledges.
11. Wait the maturation period and spend 8 Influence on her declaration.

### Position around elapsed Day 30

- Player: self + Mara + Edric + Ysabel = four prospective votes.
- Greyfen: 25% weaker economy and levy recovery.
- 40 Gold locked.
- 150 levies were unavailable during a vulnerable seven-day window.
- Claim remains Dubious.
- Church favors Renard.
- Almost all Influence has been spent.

### Counter-pressure

Renard now recognizes the player as the likely winner and can:

- attack Greyfen while military aid is locked;
- expose or manufacture legitimacy pressure;
- court Oswin and try to create a pledge-breaking shock;
- threaten Ysabel's viability calculation;
- encourage Edric's candidacy if the coalition visibly collapses.

The player has little spare Influence to repair multiple shocks.

### Outcome tested

The coalition survives one attempted Renard attack because Edric's troops return before the battle. The player does not expand, build Claim or gain Church approval.

At the King's death:

- Player: 4 votes.
- Renard: Renard and Oswin = 2 votes.

**Result:** Crowned by Acclamation.

### Verdict

**PASS.** A focused coalition is feasible but consumes almost the full political budget, weakens Greyfen and creates a long period where the player has four votes but almost no flexibility. It is not promise spam: each supporter extracted present cost.

### Watch item

Simulation must verify that Mara–Edric–Ysabel is not always the easiest trio. Their seeded demands and AI counterplay should vary the route.

## Run B — Legitimate heir

### Objective

Use Claim, Oswin and Church endorsement to cause unpledged houses to accept the player without buying three bargains.

### Stable phase

1. Research Lineage at elapsed Day 0: -35 Gold, -12 Influence, +12 Claim on Day 6.
2. Forge Royal Descent as soon as Gold reaches 50 around Day 8: -50 Gold, -25 Influence, +25 Claim on Day 16.
3. The player reaches 47 Claim / Strong.

The player has spent nearly all early Gold and Influence and carries hidden Forgery Evidence.

### Ailing/Gravely sequence

4. Raise Taxes to fund Church activity, accepting Tax Strain.
5. Declare as soon as Influence reaches 15 after the Claim projects.
6. Patronize Church for 50 Gold.
7. Offer Oswin Church Immunities: no second Gold charge, but future Raise Taxes is 20% weaker.
8. Oswin becomes Leaning, matures and Pledges.
9. Church case reaches 6: Strong Claim 3 + Oswin Pledge 2 + Patronage 1.
10. The Church Endorses the player.
11. Oswin becomes Committed because he has publicly tied the Synod to the candidacy.

### Position

- Player public votes: self + Oswin.
- Player Claim: Strong.
- Church: Endorsed.
- Gold: nearly exhausted.
- Greyfen: Tax Strain.
- Renard: still militarily and economically stronger.
- A discovered Forgery would remove Claim, Condemn the player and shock Oswin.

### Death ballot

The remaining lords are not “bonus points.” They choose under the normal evaluation rules.

- Mara prefers the player to Renard because Renard is centralizing and the Church-backed player has not violated her Red Line.
- Ysabel judges the Endorsed candidate viable.
- Edric prefers Renard's stronger military position.

First ballot with only two declared candidates:

- Player: player, Oswin, Mara, Ysabel = 4.
- Renard: Renard, Edric = 2.

**Result:** Crowned by Acclamation with the ending subtitle The Rightful Heir.

### Verdict

**PASS.** The route can win with only one purchased Pledge because legitimacy changes unpledged decisions. It is expensive, slow and catastrophically vulnerable to one discoverable secret.

### Hostile variant

Renard discovers the Forgery on Day 39.

- Player loses 20 Claim and 10 Prestige.
- Church Condemns.
- Oswin's Red Line breaks Commitment.

The player has time for Confess and Seek Penance but not enough time and Gold to reconstruct the same route. They must pivot toward Mara, a Capital tie or defeat. This is appropriate: the route is powerful because it carries a real failure state.

## Run C — Puppetmaster into constitutional coup

### Objective

Discredit Renard, assemble only three votes and use the Capital to win a 3–3 runoff.

### Stable phase

1. Find Dirt on Renard: 30 Gold, 12 Influence, five days.
2. Gift Mara or cultivate her relationship with the second slot.
3. The guaranteed Renard secret is Questioned Paternity in this seed.

### Ailing sequence

4. Declare.
5. Expose Renard's secret.
6. Renard loses Claim, his royal-favorite presumption and Church confidence.
7. Complete Mara's Charter bargain and secure her Pledge.
8. Once Renard is no longer clearly inevitable, offer Ysabel Chancellorship and locked Gold.
9. Ysabel Pledges after maturation.

### Position

- Player public votes: self, Mara, Ysabel = 3.
- Renard public/likely votes: self, Oswin, Edric = 3.
- Church: no sole endorsement after Renard's scandal.
- Player Claim remains weak.
- Player has conserved enough Gold for one mercenary band.

### Capital move

10. In Deathbed, hire one mercenary band and March on the weakened Capital.
11. Win narrowly and assign 200 troops as garrison.
12. The player does not control enough hereditary seats for Military Acclamation.

### Succession

Final ballot is 3–3.

Tie-break chain:

1. Sole Church Endorsement: neither.
2. Capital control: player.

**Result:** Master of the Capital.

### Verdict

**PASS.** Intrigue does not directly add votes. It changes the institutional landscape enough for a coalition/Capital hybrid to work. The strategy has real failure points: Spy risk, Renard retaliation and a close Capital battle.

## Run D — Crowned by the Sword

### Objective

Ignore voluntary legitimacy and meet Military Acclamation exactly.

### Preparation

1. Raise Taxes early for liquidity, accepting lost levy recovery.
2. Conserve Influence except for declaration.
3. Declare at Ailing.
4. Reach Gravely Ill with near-cap Greyfen levies and enough Gold for one mercenary band.

### Campaign 1 — Eastvale

- Commit Greyfen levies plus one band.
- Defeat Eastvale's weak fortified defense.
- Suffer approximately 70–90 casualties.
- Assign 75 troops to occupation.
- Ysabel becomes dispossessed and may seek Edric/Renard protection.

Effects:

- Prestige rises;
- Eastvale income is denied to Ysabel;
- player receives only 25% occupation income;
- threat rises sharply;
- the player does not gain Golden Vale.

### Campaign 2 — Abbeylands

- Continue before the first contract expires or renew it.
- Defeat Abbeylands.
- Suffer another 50–80 casualties.
- Assign another 75 troops.
- Church Condemns the player.

The player now controls Greyfen, Eastvale and Abbeylands but has locked 150 troops and provoked most of the realm.

### Capital campaign

- Raise Taxes a second time or use remaining income to hire/renew a second mercenary band.
- During Deathbed, attack the 300-troop royal garrison behind Fortification 3.
- Win narrowly.
- Assign 200 surviving troops to the Capital.

### Required physical state at death

- Greyfen held.
- Eastvale occupied with at least 75.
- Abbeylands occupied with at least 75.
- Capital occupied with at least 200.

Total minimum locked: 350 troops.

### Counter-pressure

Edric and Renard both classify the player as an Existential Threat. Either can attempt to liberate an occupation or seize the Capital. The player has almost no mobile reserve and must maintain mercenary contracts.

In the tested line, the King dies before Renard's liberation campaign completes.

**Result:** Crowned by the Sword.

### Verdict

**PASS, narrow.** The route is arithmetically possible but depends on Gold timing, casualty control, contract renewal and holding three vulnerable garrisons. It does not become economically stronger after conquest.

## Run E — The late hoarder

### Objective

Test the old dominant strategy: remain irrelevant, let rivals commit, then purchase support in Deathbed.

### Position at elapsed Day 42

By doing almost nothing, the player holds roughly:

- 150+ Gold;
- 75+ Influence;
- full Greyfen levies;
- no declared candidacy;
- no mature Leanings;
- Renard with at least one hardened Pledge and Church advantage.

### Burst attempt

1. Declare on Day 42–43.
2. Open Mara and Ysabel bargains in parallel.
3. Complete collateral by Day 45.
4. Both lords become Leaning.
5. Deathbed maturation requires four full days.
6. Earliest declaration requests begin around Day 49 and finish Day 50.

### Outcomes

- If death is Day 49, neither Pledge exists.
- If death is Day 50–52, at most two Pledges exist.
- A third voluntary Pledge cannot be matured in time.
- The player's late declaration loses the final tie-break to any otherwise equal earlier candidate.
- Renard has had four weeks to harden support and can react to the new threat.

The player can still attempt coercion or an improvised Capital coup, but that requires prebuilt military leverage rather than stored political currency alone.

**Result:** Renard crowned in the baseline test.

### Verdict

**PASS after Leaning maturation.** Late entry is a dramatic gamble, not the dominant default.

## Run F — Dispossessed but elected

### Objective

Ensure loss of Greyfen is severe without automatically ending a political run.

### Position before invasion

- Player Pledged by Mara and Ysabel.
- Renard attacks Greyfen and wins.
- Player becomes Dispossessed, loses income/recovery and takes Prestige/viability shock.
- Mara grants basing rights because she remains Pledged.

### Player response

- Uses existing Gold and Influence to expose Renard's secret.
- Does not have enough troops to liberate Greyfen immediately.
- Preserves both political Pledges; Ysabel is shaken but does not cross Gravely inertia.
- At death, player reaches a 3–3 final ballot.
- Renard no longer has Church endorsement after the exposed scandal.
- Player lacks Capital control.
- Tie reaches exact Claim, where Renard would win.

The player therefore spends the remaining time securing Oswin's Neutrality through the exposed scandal and a public Church action. Oswin votes player in the final ballot, creating 4–2.

**Result:** Crowned by Council while landless.

### Verdict

**PASS.** Dispossession removes economy and military initiative but leaves a narrow political path. The player cannot treat Greyfen as irrelevant because the shock nearly broke Ysabel and removed military options.

## Pass conclusion

All intended strategic families can reach a legal victory under one constitution:

- full voluntary coalition;
- legitimacy-driven unpledged votes;
- intrigue plus constitutional leverage;
- military override;
- landless political recovery.

The tests also show that most wins are hybrids, which is desirable. No separate “route meter” is needed.

The remaining work is edge-case closure and updating the canonical numeric files with the Pass 01 amendments.
