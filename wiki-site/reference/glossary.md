# Glossary

- **Claim:** Authored legal credibility band; not a generic score.
- **Collateral:** Present Gold, troops, policy, action or shared risk applied only when a bargain is accepted.
- **Commitment:** Public support tied to shared risk and hard to break.
- **Content hash:** Stable FNV-1a diagnostic hash of the validated authored pack; not a security signature.
- **Effect ID:** Declarative identifier resolved by a typed simulation handler; never an executable content closure.
- **Intent:** One major capacity-bounded AI undertaking.
- **Leaning:** Private, fluid support known only through valid information.
- **Order:** One of the player's two simultaneous initiatives.
- **Pledge:** Public support requiring maturation, Proof and present collateral.
- **Proof:** Lord-specific evidence that a candidate has satisfied an authored political requirement.
- **Red Line:** Authored condition that excludes voluntary support or breaks it according to its contract.
- **Structured effect:** Serializable effect ID, target and data consumed by a later domain handler.
- **Under Duress:** Support that lasts only while coercive leverage remains.
- **sequenceId:** Stable tie-break for simultaneous deterministic scheduling.
